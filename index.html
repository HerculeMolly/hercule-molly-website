The Workshop Guarantee

Every wooden piece is handcrafted in Cambridgeshire from reclaimed or salvaged timber—meaning knots, old nail holes, and minor tool marks are part of the C.V., not defects. Orders are packed in recycled cardboard and dispatched via Royal Mail Tracked 48 within two working days. If something arrives damaged or simply isn't right for your shelf, send it back within 30 days for a full, quibble-free refund.

UK Shipping & Returns

Woodcraft Dispatch: All physical woodcraft items are packed in my Cambridgeshire workshop and posted within 48 hours (Monday–Friday) via Royal Mail Tracked 48. Postage is calculated honestly by weight at checkout.

Watercolour Prints & Mugs: Watercolour items link out to my official Etsy shop and are printed to order by my UK print partner, covered by Etsy's buyer protection.

Returns: Under UK Consumer Contracts Regulations (and common decency), you may return any woodcraft item within 30 days of delivery in its original condition for a full refund.

Privacy & Essential Cookies

This website uses zero analytics, advertising trackers, or behavioural profiling. Because nothing tracks you across the web, we do not bother you with a cookie consent banner.

Under UK PECR regulations, only strictly necessary browser storage is used when you actively interact with site features:

  • Shopping Basket & Checkout (Snipcart & Stripe): Stores your basket contents, delivery address, and optional customer login session so you can complete a purchase securely.
  • Local Utility Apps: Saves your preferred UK postcode locally in your browser's storage so you don't have to re-type it every time you check if it's safe to hang the washing out.
